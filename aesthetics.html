AAnti-Wrinkle Injections

Anti-Wrinkle Injections (Botulinum Toxin)

A prescription injectable used to soften dynamic expression lines, such as forehead lines and crow's feet, by relaxing the targeted muscles.

Common reasons to consult

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Crow's feet

How the doctor evaluates

The doctor reviews facial anatomy, expression lines, medical history and expectations before planning doses and areas.

DDermal Fillers

Dermal Fillers

Injectable gels, commonly hyaluronic-acid based, used to restore volume or refine contours.

Common reasons to consult

  • Under-eye hollows
  • Lip definition
  • Cheek or chin contour
  • Deep folds

How the doctor evaluates

Facial proportions, skin quality and safety factors are assessed, and the plan is discussed in detail.

Book consultation

SSkin Boosters

Skin Boosters

Micro-injections designed to improve skin hydration and quality.

Common reasons to consult

  • Dehydrated skin
  • Fine crepey lines
  • Dull skin texture

How the doctor evaluates

Skin type and goals are reviewed to decide whether skin boosters are suitable.

CChemical Peels

Chemical Peels

Controlled application of peeling agents to improve texture, tone and certain acne-related concerns.

Common reasons to consult

  • Uneven tone
  • Acne and acne marks
  • Rough texture

How the doctor evaluates

The doctor chooses the peel type and strength after checking skin type, sensitivity and pigmentation risk.

HHydraFacial

HydraFacial

A multi-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates the skin.

Common reasons to consult

  • Congested pores
  • Dull skin
  • Pre-event skin care

How the doctor evaluates

Skin condition is checked to make sure the facial is appropriate.

HHIFU

HIFU

High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound, a non-surgical treatment used for skin lifting and tightening.

Common reasons to consult

  • Mild skin laxity
  • Jawline definition
  • Early sagging

How the doctor evaluates

The degree of laxity, skin thickness and expectations are assessed before treatment.

Book consultation

MMNRF

MNRF (Microneedling Radiofrequency)

Combines fine microneedles with radiofrequency energy to stimulate the skin's repair process.

Common reasons to consult

  • Acne scars
  • Open pores
  • Skin texture

How the doctor evaluates

Scar type, skin type and downtime are discussed before deciding on sessions.

Book consultation

RRF Skin Tightening

RF Skin Tightening

Radiofrequency energy used to heat deeper skin layers to support firmness.

Common reasons to consult

  • Mild skin laxity
  • Skin firmness

How the doctor evaluates

The doctor assesses skin laxity and suitability.

Carbon Laser Peel

A laser treatment performed over a thin carbon layer, often used for oily skin and dullness.

Common reasons to consult

  • Oily skin
  • Dull skin
  • Enlarged pores

How the doctor evaluates

Skin type and pigmentation risk are reviewed before treatment.

Pico Laser Treatments

CCO2 Fractional Laser

CO2 Fractional Laser

A fractional ablative laser used for resurfacing, commonly for scars and texture.

Common reasons to consult

  • Acne scars
  • Uneven texture
  • Fine lines

How the doctor evaluates

Skin type, downtime and aftercare are discussed carefully before treatment.

Laser Skin Rejuvenation

Laser-based treatments aimed at improving overall skin quality and tone.

Common reasons to consult

  • Uneven tone
  • Texture

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

Uses a concentrate prepared from the patient's own blood, applied for selected skin and hair concerns.

Common reasons to consult

  • Skin quality

Common reasons to consult

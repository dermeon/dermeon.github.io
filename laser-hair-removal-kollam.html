Laser Hair Removal at DERMEON

What to know

Laser hair reduction targets hair follicles to reduce unwanted hair over a series of sessions. At DERMEON it is done with a diode laser after a doctor checks your skin type, hair type and any hormonal cause of excess hair.

Questions

Laser Hair Removal – FAQs

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser reduces hair significantly for many people, but some hair can regrow. Maintenance sessions may be needed.

How many sessions are needed?

Most people need several sessions spaced a few weeks apart. The number depends on area, hair type and hormones.

Is it safe for dark skin?

Diode lasers can be used on Indian skin types when settings are chosen carefully by trained staff under a doctor's supervision.

What should I avoid before a session?

Avoid waxing, threading and sun tanning before sessions. Shaving is usually allowed. Your clinic will give exact instructions.

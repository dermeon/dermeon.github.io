About DERMEON

A dermatology, medical cosmetology and aesthetic medicine clinic in Asramam, Kollam. A unit of Chaithram Healthcare.

Introduction

Advanced dermatology. Refined aesthetics.

DERMEON is a doctor-led clinic for skin, hair and aesthetic care. Our team includes two dermatologists and a managing director who is a medical doctor and aesthetic physician. Patients come to us for medical dermatology, cosmetology procedures, laser treatments and hair care.

Our vision

To be a trusted name in Kollam for dermatology and aesthetic medicine, where patients receive careful, honest and modern care.

Our mission

To diagnose accurately, explain clearly and treat safely, using treatment plans that respect each patient's skin, health and goals.

Clinical philosophy

We believe good aesthetic results come from good medicine. A correct diagnosis comes before any procedure, and we recommend treatment only when it is suitable.

Patient-centred care

We listen first. Your concerns, expectations, budget and time are part of every plan, and we explain sessions, aftercare and possible side effects before you decide.

Medical expertise

Our dermatologists manage conditions such as acne, pigmentation, allergies, hair loss and scalp disorders. Our aesthetic physician leads injectable and skin-quality treatments.

Advanced infrastructure

The clinic has dedicated consultation and treatment rooms and modern laser and aesthetic equipment.

Safety and hygiene

We follow clinical hygiene practices for consultations and procedures, and patients receive aftercare instructions for every treatment.

Personalised treatment planning

No two patients are the same. Your plan may combine medical treatment, skin care advice and procedures, and is reviewed at follow-up visits.

Begin with a consultation

Every treatment plan at DERMEON starts with a doctor's assessment of your skin or hair.

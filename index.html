Treatments in Kollam

Find care for your concern

Acne Treatment

Acne is a medical skin condition, not just a cosmetic problem.

Acne Scar Treatment

Acne scars form when deep acne damages the skin.

Pigmentation & Melasma

Dark patches can have many causes, including sun exposure, hormones, skin products and inflammation.

Hair Fall Treatment

Hair fall has many causes, including genetics, stress, thyroid problems, low iron or vitamin levels, hormones and scalp conditions.

Laser Hair Removal

Laser hair reduction targets hair follicles to reduce unwanted hair over a series of sessions.

Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine that relaxes selected facial muscles to soften lines caused by expression.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers are injectable gels, commonly made of hyaluronic acid, used to restore lost volume or refine facial contours.

Skin Tightening

Non-surgical tightening uses energy, such as focused ultrasound (HIFU) or radiofrequency, to heat deeper skin layers and support firmness.

HydraFacial

A HydraFacial is a multi-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates the skin.

Chemical Peels

A chemical peel uses a controlled solution to remove the outer layer of skin, helping with acne, uneven tone and texture.

Tattoo Removal

Laser tattoo removal breaks tattoo ink into small particles that the body clears over time.

Skin Allergy & Eczema

Itching, redness and rashes can be caused by eczema, allergy to products or metals, infections and other conditions.

Warts, Skin Tags & Moles

Warts, skin tags and small growths on the face and neck are common.

Dark Circles

Dark circles have different causes: pigmentation, thin skin showing blood vessels, hollowness, allergy or lifestyle factors.

Weight Management

Weight gain can be linked to thyroid problems, PCOS, insulin resistance, medicines and lifestyle.

Dandruff & Scalp Care

Persistent dandruff and an itchy scalp are often due to seborrhoeic dermatitis or other scalp conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Before your visit

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Yes. Every procedure at DERMEON begins with a doctor's consultation, so that the treatment is suitable for your skin, hair and health.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the online form?

No. The online form sends an appointment request. Our team will call or WhatsApp you to confirm the date and time.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the condition, skin type and treatment chosen. Your doctor will explain the expected number of sessions after examining you.

Are results guaranteed?

No medical or aesthetic treatment can guarantee results. Outcomes vary between individuals, and your doctor will discuss realistic expectations with you.

Where is DERMEON located?

DERMEON is at Asramam, Kollam, Kerala – 691002. You can find directions on our Contact page.

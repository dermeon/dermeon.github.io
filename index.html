Frequently asked questions

Before your visit

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Yes. Every procedure at DERMEON begins with a doctor's consultation, so that the treatment is suitable for your skin, hair and health.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the online form?

No. The online form sends an appointment request. Our team will call or WhatsApp you to confirm the date and time.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the condition, skin type and treatment chosen. Your doctor will explain the expected number of sessions after examining you.

Are results guaranteed?

No medical or aesthetic treatment can guarantee results. Outcomes vary between individuals, and your doctor will discuss realistic expectations with you.

Where is DERMEON located?

DERMEON is at Asramam, Kollam, Kerala – 691002. You can find directions on our Contact page.

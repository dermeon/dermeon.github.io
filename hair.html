HHair Loss Consultation

Hair Loss Consultation

A doctor's consultation to understand why hair fall is happening.

Common reasons to consult

  • Increased hair fall
  • Thinning or receding hairline
  • Patchy hair loss

How the doctor evaluates

  • History, diet, stress and medicines
  • Family history
  • Blood tests where needed

Scalp Assessment

Examination of the scalp and hair to identify the pattern and cause of hair loss.

Common reasons to consult

  • Thinning areas
  • Breakage

How the doctor evaluates

  • Scalp and hair examination

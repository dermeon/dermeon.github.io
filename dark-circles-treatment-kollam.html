Dark Circles at DERMEON

What to know

Dark circles have different causes: pigmentation, thin skin showing blood vessels, hollowness, allergy or lifestyle factors. Treatment depends on the cause, so the doctor examines the area first.

Questions

Dark Circles – FAQs

Why do I have dark circles even with enough sleep?

Genetics, pigmentation, thin skin and hollows can cause dark circles regardless of sleep.

Are under-eye fillers safe?

When chosen for the right patient and given by a trained doctor, they are widely used. The doctor will explain the risks.

Will creams alone work?

Creams help pigmentation-type dark circles. Hollows or thin skin may need other treatment.

How long before I see improvement?

It depends on the treatment. The doctor will review progress at follow-up.

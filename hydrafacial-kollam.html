Questions

HydraFacial – FAQs

Is HydraFacial suitable for acne-prone skin?

It can help congested skin, but active or severe acne may need medical treatment first. The doctor will advise.

When should I do a facial before my wedding?

Plan facials a few weeks ahead so the skin settles. The clinic can suggest a timeline.

Is there any downtime?

Usually none. Mild redness may last a short time.

How often can I do it?

Your doctor will suggest a frequency based on your skin.

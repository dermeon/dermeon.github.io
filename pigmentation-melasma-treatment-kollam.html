Pigmentation & Melasma at DERMEON

What to know

Dark patches can have many causes, including sun exposure, hormones, skin products and inflammation. Melasma in particular needs careful, long-term care. Our doctors identify the type of pigmentation before choosing treatment.

Questions

Pigmentation & Melasma – FAQs

Can melasma be cured permanently?

Melasma can be controlled and lightened, but it can come back, especially with sun exposure. Maintenance care is important.

Is laser safe for Indian skin?

Laser can be used on Indian skin when settings are chosen carefully. The doctor decides whether laser suits your pigmentation.

Why does my pigmentation keep coming back?

Sun exposure, heat, hormones and some products can trigger it again. The doctor will help you identify your triggers.

Do I need sunscreen every day?

Yes. Daily sun protection is one of the most important parts of pigmentation treatment.

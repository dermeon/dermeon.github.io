Weight Management at DERMEON

What to know

Weight gain can be linked to thyroid problems, PCOS, insulin resistance, medicines and lifestyle. A medical weight-management plan starts with assessment and tests, then sets realistic, safe goals with regular follow-up.

Questions

Weight Management – FAQs

Is weight-loss medicine safe?

Some medicines can help selected people but have side effects. They are prescribed only after a doctor's assessment.

Do I need blood tests?

Tests such as thyroid, sugar and hormone levels may be advised to find medical causes.

How much weight can I lose?

It varies from person to person. The doctor will set realistic and safe goals with you.

Can fat be reduced from one area only?

Non-surgical body contouring may help selected areas, but it is not a substitute for weight loss. The doctor will advise.

Skin conditions

Acne and Pimple Treatment in Kollam

Pigmentation and Melasma Treatment in Kollam

Dark patches can have many causes, including sun exposure, hormones, skin products and inflammation.

Skin Allergy, Eczema and Itching Treatment in Kollam

Itching, redness and rashes can be caused by eczema, allergy to products or metals, infections and other conditions.

Wart, Skin Tag and Mole Removal in Kollam

Hair and scalp

Hair Fall Treatment in Kollam

Hair fall has many causes, including genetics, stress, thyroid problems, low iron or vitamin levels, hormones and scalp conditions.

Dandruff and Scalp Treatment in Kollam

Persistent dandruff and an itchy scalp are often due to seborrhoeic dermatitis or other scalp conditions.

Laser

Laser Hair Removal in Kollam

Laser hair reduction targets hair follicles to reduce unwanted hair over a series of sessions.

Laser Tattoo Removal in Kollam

Acne Scar Treatment in Kollam

Aesthetic medicine

Anti-Wrinkle Injections (Botulinum Toxin) in Kollam

Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine that relaxes selected facial muscles to soften lines caused by expression.

Dermal Fillers in Kollam

Dermal fillers are injectable gels, commonly made of hyaluronic acid, used to restore lost volume or refine facial contours.

Skin Tightening Treatment in Kollam (HIFU and RF)

Non-surgical tightening uses energy, such as focused ultrasound (HIFU) or radiofrequency, to heat deeper skin layers and support firmness.

HydraFacial and Medi-Facials in Kollam

A HydraFacial is a multi-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates the skin.

Chemical Peel Treatment in Kollam

A chemical peel uses a controlled solution to remove the outer layer of skin, helping with acne, uneven tone and texture.

Chemical Peels at DERMEON

What to know

A chemical peel uses a controlled solution to remove the outer layer of skin, helping with acne, uneven tone and texture. The type and strength of peel are chosen by a doctor, because the wrong peel can cause irritation or darkening.

Questions

Chemical Peels – FAQs

Will my skin peel a lot?

It depends on the peel. Some cause little visible peeling, others cause flaking for a few days.

Can I go out in the sun after a peel?

Sun protection is essential after a peel. The doctor will give aftercare instructions.

How many peel sessions do I need?

Usually a series of sessions. The number depends on your concern and skin.

Are peels safe for darker skin?

Yes, when the right peel and strength are chosen by a doctor.

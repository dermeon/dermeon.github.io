Dermal Fillers at DERMEON

What to know

Dermal fillers are injectable gels, commonly made of hyaluronic acid, used to restore lost volume or refine facial contours. Because the face has important blood vessels, fillers must be planned and injected by a trained doctor.

Questions

Dermal Fillers – FAQs

Are fillers permanent?

Hyaluronic acid fillers are temporary and are slowly absorbed by the body over months.

Can fillers be reversed?

Most hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if needed.

Is there swelling after fillers?

Mild swelling or bruising for a few days is common. The doctor will give aftercare advice.

Who should not have fillers?

People with active skin infection in the area, some medical conditions or pregnancy may be advised to wait. The doctor will check suitability.

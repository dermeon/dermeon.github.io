Questions

Dandruff & Scalp Care – FAQs

Is dandruff a fungal infection?

Dandruff is linked to yeast on the scalp and the skin's reaction to it. The doctor will choose the right treatment.

Does dandruff cause hair fall?

Scalp inflammation can make hair fall worse. Treating the scalp helps overall hair health.

How often should I wash my hair?

It depends on your scalp and the treatment. The doctor will advise a routine.

Why does dandruff come back?

It is often a long-term tendency. Maintenance care helps keep it under control.

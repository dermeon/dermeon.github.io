AAcne and Acne Scar Management

Acne and Acne Scar Management

Medical assessment and treatment of active acne, the marks it leaves behind and the textural scars that can follow.

Common reasons to consult

  • Persistent pimples, blackheads or whiteheads
  • Painful cystic or nodular acne
  • Dark marks after acne
  • Pitted or uneven acne scars

How the doctor evaluates

  • Type, severity and pattern of acne
  • Skin type and current skin-care routine
  • Medicines, hormones or lifestyle factors that may contribute
  • Scar type, to decide whether procedures are suitable

PPigmentation and Melasma

Pigmentation and Melasma

Evaluation of uneven skin tone, dark patches and melasma, with a plan that may combine medical care, sun protection and procedures.

Common reasons to consult

  • Brown or grey patches on the face
  • Uneven skin tone or tanning
  • Dark spots after acne or injury
  • Pigmentation around the eyes or neck

How the doctor evaluates

  • Pattern and depth of pigmentation
  • Triggers such as sun exposure, hormones or products
  • Skin sensitivity before any procedure
  • Long-term maintenance needs

Book consultation

SSkin Allergy and Dermatitis

Skin Allergy and Dermatitis

Diagnosis and management of itchy, red or inflamed skin, including eczema and contact dermatitis.

Common reasons to consult

  • Itching, redness or rashes
  • Dry, scaly or cracked skin
  • Reactions to cosmetics, metals or chemicals
  • Recurring eczema flares

How the doctor evaluates

  • Detailed history of symptoms and triggers
  • Examination of the affected skin
  • Review of products and exposures
  • Tests where clinically needed

Book consultation

HHair Fall and Scalp Disorders

Hair Fall and Scalp Disorders

Assessment of increased hair shedding, thinning and conditions affecting the scalp.

Common reasons to consult

  • Increased hair fall or thinning
  • Widening parting or receding hairline
  • Patchy hair loss
  • Itchy or painful scalp

How the doctor evaluates

  • Pattern and duration of hair loss
  • Scalp examination
  • Health, nutrition and medicine history
  • Blood tests where clinically needed

Book consultation

DDandruff and Scalp Care

Dandruff and Scalp Care

Medical care for flaking, itching and oily or dry scalp conditions.

Common reasons to consult

  • Persistent flakes
  • Itchy or oily scalp
  • Redness along the hairline
  • Dandruff returning after treatment

How the doctor evaluates

  • Type of scaling and scalp condition
  • Hair-care habits and products
  • Related skin conditions
  • Suitable medicated care

Book consultation

SSkin Rejuvenation

Skin Rejuvenation

Doctor-guided plans to improve skin texture, glow and early signs of ageing.

Common reasons to consult

  • Dull or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and early ageing
  • Uneven texture or open pores
  • Preparing skin for an occasion

How the doctor evaluates

  • Skin type and concerns
  • Lifestyle and sun exposure
  • Suitability for peels, lasers or injectables
  • A realistic, staged plan

Book consultation

OOther Medical Dermatology Services

Other Medical Dermatology Services

Consultation for a wide range of skin, hair and nail conditions.

Common reasons to consult

  • Warts, skin tags and moles
  • Fungal and bacterial skin infections
  • Nail problems
  • Psoriasis, vitiligo and other chronic conditions

How the doctor evaluates

  • Clinical examination
  • History of the condition
  • Investigations where needed
  • Treatment and follow-up plan

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

When you request an appointment we collect your name, mobile and WhatsApp number, optional email address, the department and treatment you are interested in, your preferred doctor, date and time, and any message you write. We do not ask for medical records through this website.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you about your appointment request, to schedule your visit and for related follow-up by phone, SMS or WhatsApp.

Storage and access

Appointment requests are stored in a secure account of the clinic and are accessible only to authorised clinic staff.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Patient photographs and reviews

We do not publish patient photographs or personal information on this website without the patient's permission.

Your choices

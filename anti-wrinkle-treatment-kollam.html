Anti-Wrinkle Injections at DERMEON

What to know

Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine that relaxes selected facial muscles to soften lines caused by expression. At DERMEON the injections are planned and given by a doctor after a detailed facial assessment.

Questions

Anti-Wrinkle Injections – FAQs

Is botulinum toxin safe?

When given by a trained doctor in the correct dose, it is widely used. Side effects such as bruising or a temporary droop can occur, and the doctor will explain them.

How long does it last?

The effect usually lasts a few months and then wears off gradually.

Will I look frozen?

The aim is to soften lines while keeping natural expression. The doctor plans the dose with this in mind.

Is Botox the same as botulinum toxin?

Botox is one brand of botulinum toxin. Different brands are available, and the doctor will explain the product used.
